# Lifespan Cognition Lab Schedule

A week-by-week calendar of the times lab members can't be in the lab, hosted on GitHub Pages. Members add their unavailable times (classes, other commitments and time off), and any time they haven't added counts as available. Use the arrows or the small month calendar to move between weeks, months and years. It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step.

## Using it

Sign in with the Google account your lab reviewer added. There are three roles: **RAs** and **Grad Students/Post-Docs** see only their own schedule, and **Reviewers** see everyone's and manage the lab. RAs' changes to saved times and their time off go to a reviewer for approval; Grad Students/Post-Docs' save straight away, and reviewers are emailed about them. The top bar switches between **Calendar** and **Update Availability**, and reviewers also see **Lab Management**. You only see your own unavailable times; reviewers can see everyone's.

- **Class schedule:** pick the semester, then upload a PDF or screenshot, or enter times by hand. The file is read in your browser and is never uploaded. Check the times it found, then save. If your schedule changes partway through, choose **A change to my schedule partway through**: your earlier classes stay, and the new times start on the date you pick.
- **Standing conflict:** regular commitments outside class, which stay on the calendar through holidays and breaks.
- **Request time off:** whole days, or part of a single day, with a required Notes/Reason. For RAs it goes to a reviewer, and shows as pending on your own calendar until it's approved. Editing an approved request sends it back for review.
- **My availability:** edit or remove anything you've saved, and see the status of your requests.
- **On the calendar:** click your own blocks to change or remove them, drag a block to change its time (drag its bottom edge to change when it ends), or drag across empty space to add a time. You choose whether it repeats every week (until a date, until the last day of classes, or with no end) or happens once. Repeating times save straight away; one-time times are sent as a time off request for that part of the day. Changes to times you've already saved go to a reviewer, and your saved times stay in place until the change is approved. You can cancel a change while it's waiting.
- **Lab Events** (reviewers only): reviewers' calendar opens on **Lab Events**, which shows participant visits; switch to **Unavailability** to see everyone's unavailable times. Every weekday that isn't a USC staff holiday gets a default visit, added 60 days ahead at 8:00 AM each morning. A visit is a *Participant Visit - Lab* slot (9–11 AM: Dry Lab for the whole slot; Phlebotomy for 30 minutes at any point in the slot; Wet Lab for 2 hours starting within 30 minutes after phlebotomy ends, which can run past the slot; Wet Lab and Dry Lab done by different people; phlebotomy goes to the most experienced level free: with no Experienced Phlebotomist the slot is amber with a note that mobile phlebotomy may need to be on stand-by, or booked in advance for difficult veins, and with only a Training Phlebotomist it's red, since it can only go ahead if mobile phlebotomy is booked in advance) and a *Participant Visit - Scan* slot (11 AM–1 PM; two scanners, with Auxiliary Support Scanning paired with Level 2 Scanning, someone with Level 2 Scanning outside weekdays 8 AM–5 PM, and someone for wanding/taping unless that's turned off for the visit). A full visit lasts 4 hours from its start even when the scan is earlier or the lab portion later than usual: lab staff only come for their own part, and a lab manager (anyone with the Reviewer role) finishes the visit after the scan. Lab managers should cover the whole visit between them, handing off if needed; if one is only free for the wrap-up at the end the visit is amber, and with none for the wrap-up it's red. Times other than a 9:00 AM lab start and an 11:00 AM, 2-hour scan are marked **🕒 [SEE TIME]** everywhere the visit appears. Each slot lists who's trained and free; red slots can't be fully staffed and say why, and amber slots only work with a particular timing, which the slot spells out, have no Experienced Phlebotomist, or have gaps in lab manager cover. An asterisk marks someone who's only free for part of the time, with details below the names. Click a slot to change its time, turn off wanding/taping, mark it booked, staff it, or cancel it (cancelling asks for a second click); drag to move it; drag across empty space to add a full visit, or one part for participants who only come in for that. Visits reviewers add are marked booked unless they untick **Confirmed booking**. When a day has more than one visit they're numbered, and cancelling one never cancels another. Cancelled default visits show a **Restore** link.
- **Staffing a visit** (reviewers only): **Staff this visit** suggests who should do each role, using the most experienced phlebotomist available and then whoever has the fewest visits within four weeks, so people rotate fairly. A few alternatives are listed for each part, and any role can be changed by hand; the dialog checks the result as you go. **Approve staffing** saves it, and the Apps Script puts it on the **LCL Confirmed Visits** calendar. Staffed visits show **Staffed** on the calendar, or **Staffing conflict** if someone assigned is no longer free. Moving the lab portion moves phlebotomy and Wet Lab with it.
- **Booking** (reviewers, and anyone given the **Booking** responsibility under Lab members): lists visits from Jan 3, 2027 on, with their times and whether they're booked, but not who is staffing them. Bookers mark a visit booked, or cancel a booking when a participant cancels, which keeps the visit open so it can be rebooked, emails the lab managers, and doesn't tell its staff. **Participant information never goes on the site**, not even a Screener ID. Marking a visit booked opens an email to the reviewers in the booker's own USC email, with the visit's times filled in and a blank Screener ID line for the booker to complete. It reminds reviewers to create the confirmed appointment, with the participant information, in the lab's own appointment calendar in Google Calendar, and links to that visit's suggested staffing on the site. Approving the staffing there confirms the visit and queues the staff calendar invites and emails. Cancelling a booking emails the lab managers automatically. If the booking email doesn't open, **Email booking details to reviewers** on the visit does the same. Time off that's still waiting for review counts as unavailable.
- **My Profile and the Lab Directory**: the first time someone signs in, they fill in their phone number, date of birth, expected graduation (month and year, or N/A for non-students), dietary restrictions/allergies, favorite color and favorite kind of dessert, and can change any of it later under **My Profile**. **Directory** lists everyone in the lab with their directory role (RA, PhD Student, Post-Doc, Lab Manager or PI, set by lab managers on each person's card under Lab members), email and phone number. Lab managers also see a table of everyone's birthday, expected graduation, favorites and dietary restrictions/allergies, which nobody else can see.
- **Lab Management** (reviewers only), with **Review requests**, **Who's available**, **Lab members** and **My information** tabs. **Who's available** shows, for any day and times, who in each role is free the whole time or part of it, and can test the times as a full visit, lab portion or scan to see whether they could be staffed. The other tabs: approve or decline requests, and manage who has access. Removing someone also deletes their schedule. **Edit schedule** next to a person opens **Update Availability** for them: reviewers can pick anyone under **Entering times for** and add, change or remove their classes, conflicts and time off, which saves straight away without review (time off entered this way counts as approved). Reviewers can also click or drag anyone's blocks on the Unavailability calendar. Each person's training (Wet Lab, Dry Lab, a phlebotomy level of Experienced Phlebotomist, Phlebotomist or Training Phlebotomist, Level 1 Scanning, Level 2 Scanning, Auxiliary Support Scanning, Wanding/Taping, and the Booking responsibility) can be changed at any time under **Lab members**. Reviewers get emails about requests, cancellations and scans (see **Reviewer emails** below), and choose which under **My information**.

Reading works best when each class has its days and times written out as text, such as `MWF 9:00-9:50 AM`. Calendar-style screenshots that show classes only as colored blocks have no times to read, so those need to be entered by hand.

## USC dates

Semesters, holidays and breaks are in [`data/academic-calendar.json`](data/academic-calendar.json), copied from the [USC academic calendar](https://usc.edu/academic-calendar/) through Summer 2029. Add later years there as USC publishes them. Each "Break between terms" is the gap between official terms, not a name USC uses. Some USC schools publish their own calendars, which can differ.

Class times only appear from the first to the last day of classes, and not on holidays or breaks. Study days and final exams are labeled, but regular class times aren't shown on them.

## USC staff holidays

Participant visits skip the dates in [`data/staff-holidays.json`](data/staff-holidays.json). They follow USC's Paid University Holidays policy (New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas) plus winter recess. A Saturday holiday is observed the Friday before and a Sunday holiday the Monday after. USC sometimes adds a bridge day when a holiday falls on a Tuesday or Thursday, so add those to the file when they're announced. The 2026 winter break runs Dec 17, 2026 to Jan 10, 2027 for staff, matching the student break. Visits still run during student-only breaks.

## How it's set up

This repository is public and holds only the site's code and USC's public dates. Schedules live in a private Firebase database. The site's code runs in people's browsers, so **[`firestore.rules`](firestore.rules) is what actually enforces privacy**. After any change to that file, paste it into the Firebase console (Firestore Database > Rules) and publish it.

The values in `firebase-config.js` identify the project and are meant to be public. Never commit a service account key.

Setup:

1. Create a project in the [Firebase console](https://console.firebase.google.com/) and add a Web app. Copy its values into `firebase-config.js`.
2. Under **Authentication > Sign-in method**, enable **Google**. Under **Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains**, add `dbelkind.github.io`.
3. Create a **Firestore Database** in production mode, and paste in `firestore.rules` under **Rules**.
4. Add the first reviewer by hand: create a collection named `members` and a document whose ID is their Google email in lowercase, with the string fields `name` and `role` set to `reviewer`. Add everyone else from the site.

## Google Calendar and email

[`apps-script/Code.gs`](apps-script/Code.gs) is a Google Apps Script that keeps four calendars in your Google Calendar, covering the next 60 days, and sends the lab's emails. It reads the schedule with your own Google login, so it must run under the account that owns the Firebase project (dbelkind@usc.edu), and it loads the staffing rules from the published site, so push site changes to GitHub before expecting them in the calendars. Its calendar permission only covers calendars the script creates itself, so it can't see or change your other calendars, and it can't share its own.

- **LCL Visits**: one event for each participant visit, for the whole visit. Its color is its booking from Jan 2027 on: gray when open, blue when booked, orange when a booking was cancelled and it's open to rebook (fall visits keep the calendar's own color). Its title starts ✅, 🟡 or ⚠️ for whether it can be staffed, says why if not, and ends "staffed" once staffing is approved. The description lists who's available for each part and the lab manager, and links to the visit's staffing on the site. It updates every 10 minutes, and straight away after a booking, visit or staffing change on the site (see **Instant updates** below). This was the Lab Staffing calendar, which is renamed rather than replaced.
- **LCL Confirmed Visits**: each staffed visit, as two events: one for the whole visit (4 hours from its start) listing the lab staff and lab manager with each person's own times, and one for the scan listing the scanners. Updated every 10 minutes.
- **LCL Unavailability**: when each lab member can't be in the lab (classes, standing conflicts and time off), each person in their own color. Updated every hour.
- **LCL Birthdays & Graduations**: everyone's birthday and expected graduation for the coming year, as all-day events with their favorite color, dessert and dietary restrictions/allergies. A graduation is marked on the first of its month, since only the month is known. Updated every hour.

Share all four with each lab manager, and only lab managers: in Google Calendar, open each calendar's **Settings and sharing → Share with specific people** and choose **See all event details**. Bookers don't need them; they use the Booking page. LCL Unavailability is busy, so each person can untick it in their calendar list and turn it on when they need it.

Setup, once:

1. Go to [script.google.com](https://script.google.com) signed in as dbelkind@usc.edu and create a **New project**. Name it "Lab Staffing Sync".
2. Under **Project Settings** (the gear), tick **Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor**.
3. Back in the **Editor**, replace the contents of `appsscript.json` with [`apps-script/appsscript.json`](apps-script/appsscript.json), and the contents of `Code.gs` with [`apps-script/Code.gs`](apps-script/Code.gs). Save.
4. Choose **setUp** in the function menu at the top and click **Run**. Google asks for permission; because it's your own script it may say Google hasn't verified it, so choose **Advanced**, then **Go to Lab Staffing Sync**, and allow.
5. The four calendars appear in Google Calendar within a minute.

Run **setUp** again whenever you paste in a new `Code.gs`. To see what it did, open **Executions** in the Apps Script sidebar. Only events the script made are changed; anything you add to its calendars by hand is left alone. Edit visits on the website, not in Google Calendar, since the next run puts the calendars back in line with the site.

**Instant updates.** The site can ask the script to update LCL Visits and send cancellation emails straight away, rather than at the next 10-minute run. Staff invites and emails still wait for that run on purpose, so a mistake fixed in the meantime never reaches anyone. To turn it on: in Apps Script choose **Deploy → New deployment**, pick **Web app**, set **Execute as: Me** and **Who has access: Anyone**, and deploy. Copy the web app URL into `SYNC_URL` in [`firebase-config.js`](firebase-config.js) and push. The site sends it nothing but the request to sync, and the script runs at most once every few seconds, so the link is harmless if anyone else finds it. If USC doesn't allow "Anyone", the calendars simply keep updating every 10 minutes. After changing `Code.gs`, use **Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit → New version** so the web app runs the new code.

**Staff emails.** **Switched off for now** (`inviteStaff: false` at the top of `Code.gs`): staff are only listed in the LCL Confirmed Visits descriptions, not added as guests, and nobody but reviewers is emailed. Once it's switched on, lab staff and lab managers are added to the visit event and scanners to the scan event, and every 10 minutes the script emails each person who's affected, and only them, about what changed for them: assigned (with their roles and times), times changed (before and now), taken off, or the visit cancelled, with 🕒 [SEE TIME] when the times aren't the usual ones. Google itself never emails guests. A cancelled booking that's waiting to be rebooked doesn't change anything, so staff only hear about a cancellation once a reviewer cancels the visit on the site. Staff can't turn these emails off.

**Reviewer emails.** Emails go to all the reviewers at once rather than one each, so the account the script runs under doesn't collect copies of every reviewer's email, and nobody is emailed about something they did themselves. Each reviewer picks which kinds they get under **Lab Management → My information → Email alerts**; a reviewer who turns a kind off is simply left off those emails.

- **Requests to review**: new time off requests and schedule changes, once per request, linking to **Review requests**. The first check after setup only notes what's already waiting.
- **Saved schedule changes**: changes and time off Grad Students/Post-Docs saved without review.
- **Cancellations**: straight away when a booking is cancelled (the visit stays open to rebook, and its staff aren't told), or when a booked or staffed visit is cancelled on the site. Visits being booked aren't emailed by the script: the booker emails the reviewers from their own account, with the Screener ID.
- **Scan booking reminder**: at 8:30 AM, which newly opened visit needs its scan booked.
- **Birthdays and graduations**: at 8:30 AM, 1 month, 1 week and 1 day before each birthday and expected graduation, and on the morning of, with the person's favorite color, dessert and dietary restrictions/allergies. Nobody is reminded about their own.

## Trying it locally

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000. If port 8000 is taken, use a different number.

After changing `style.css` or any of the site's `.js` files, raise the `?v=` number on its link in `index.html`, so browsers don't keep using their cached copy.
