Booking

Participant visits open for booking. A new day opens at 8:00 AM each morning, 60 days ahead. A full visit lasts 4 hours from its start; check the times on any visit marked 🕒 [SEE TIME].

Never enter participant information on this site, not even a Screener ID. When you mark a visit booked, an email to the reviewers opens in your own USC email: add the Screener ID and send it. If it doesn't open, use Email booking details to reviewers. If a participant cancels, use Cancel booking: the visit stays open so it can be rebooked, and the lab managers are emailed automatically.
